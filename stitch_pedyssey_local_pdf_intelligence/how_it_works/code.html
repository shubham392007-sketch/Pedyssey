The Process

How Pedyssey turns pages into answers.

We've engineered a seamless, local-first pipeline that respects your privacy while delivering unparalleled intellectual insights from your documents.

01

Upload

Drop your PDFs into the local workspace. Files never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy.

02

Process

Our local engine extracts text, structures, and metadata, preparing the document for deep analysis.

03

Understand

The AI builds a semantic map of your text, grasping context, nuances, and core arguments.

04

Retrieve

Ask a question, and Pedyssey rapidly locates the most relevant passages across your entire library.

05

Reason

The AI synthesizes the retrieved information, drawing connections and formulating a logical response.

06

Answer

You receive a clear, cited answer, beautifully presented and ready for your academic or professional work.
